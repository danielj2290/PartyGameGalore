# Charades word bank

Edit the pipe-separated prompts in `words.ts`. Each `|` starts a new card. There are 500 unique prompts: 250 Easy and 250 Challenging. All 300 previous prompts, including manual edits, remain in the bank.

- Easy combines the previous Easy bank, 50 concrete or familiar Medium prompts, and 100 additions. The promoted Medium prompts include everyday objects, recognizable animals, routine actions, and familiar characters.
- Challenging combines the other 50 Medium prompts, the previous Hard bank, and 100 additions. These need more invention, subtler expressions, or several actions to communicate.
- New cards focus on physical comedy, awkward mishaps, facial reactions, and silly character combinations. User requests include Constipation, Broken leg, Eating jumbo sandwich, and the existing Clown card. Most additions are one to five words.
- `expressions` is additional internal metadata. Category filtering is still a future Charades feature.

## Inspiration checked October 18, 2026

These public pages were read for ideas about physical comedy and acting feelings. New prompts use short wording and original combinations rather than reproducing an article's list or instructions.

- [Charades.io: Funny Charades Ideas](https://charades.io/funny-charades-ideas/) — everyday mishaps and unusual character situations.
- [National Braille Press: Speaking without Words](https://www.nbp.org/ic/nbp/programs/gep/grumpy/grumpy-speak.html) — readable emotions, facial expressions, and body language.
- [Friends of the Groom: Mime Emotion](https://www.friendsofthegroom.org/mimeemotion) — silently communicating emotions through face and body.
